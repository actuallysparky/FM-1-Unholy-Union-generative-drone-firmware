Browser & connection notes
Use desktop Chrome or Edge on HTTPS or localhost with Web MIDI SysEx permission. Firefox lacks this transport; Safari and mobile browsers are not supported here. This installer uses Web MIDI, not WebUSB. Connect only one FM-1 and leave it connected throughout the update. Close music apps using the same MIDI ports. No device permission is requested on page load.
Stock V14 (FM-1_014) and V15 (FM-1_015) are accepted. V15 installation has been confirmed on one FM-1. V16 and unlisted firmware remain unsupported by this offering.
Recovery if an update is interrupted
Keep power and USB stable. If the upstream update loader still appears, a reviewed package may be retried through the upstream installer’s resume path. Resume starts the loader transfer again; it is not a byte-offset continuation.
A missing loader, wrong identity, or lost return is an error—not success. Recovery outside that loader is unverified. Do not keep retrying arbitrary packages or erase flash. Preserve the event log and get device-specific recovery guidance.

What we preserved
Felucca’s engines, four tracks, shared eight-voice budget, sequencing, sampling, MIDI, editor, and flash allocations. New drift and macros use appended source IDs; a new project marker prevents silent backward misreading.

THE SAINTS BEHIND THE SOUND
Sing their praises. Sustain the note.
Saint Felucca
Felucca, by Leo Kuroshita (@kurogedelic), Hügelton Instruments and its contributors, is our foundation: engines, sequencing, modulation, USB/MIDI, storage, updater and editor. Their voices carry this congregation.
Saint Jangada
Jangada, by zednaked and its contributors, gave us the drone algorithm adapted for slowly evolving sustain and tension. Patron saint of the note that refuses to leave.
Archangel Chance
Cyma Forma and Bambounou’s RND synth inspired the one-button invitation behind Immaculate Permutation. May their random wonders ring through every sacred valley.
Felucca and Jangada are our code foundations. RND is design inspiration; no RND code or assets were copied. Unaffiliated fork, with gratitude to every contributor. GPL-3.0-only.
